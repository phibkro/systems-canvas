# System Canvas — Expert Collaboration Brief

## Purpose

Invite critique and co-development of a **provisional** diagnostic, redesign and teaching method—not endorsement of a new universal formal theory.

## Where expert review is needed

| Expertise | Questions / proposed contribution |
|---|---|
| Systems / cybernetics | Correct feedback, viability and recursive-governance procedures |
| Institutional research | Compare with Ostrom IAD, VSM and 4EM; improve authority mapping |
| Game theory | Identify when strategic modeling adds value and when it misleads |
| Epistemology and journalism | Source provenance, uncertainty, misinformation and corrections |
| Causal inference | Detect unfounded arrows, design tests and counterfactuals |
| HCI and education | Visual grammar, progressive teaching and independent transfer |
| Ethics / political theory | Legitimacy, conflicting goals, power and affected-party inclusion |

## Specific requests

1. Which theoretical distinctions are mistaken, redundant or missing?
2. What established method already does the same job?
3. Which procedures can be derived without heavy mathematical prerequisites?
4. How can novices distinguish a genuine mechanism from a coherent narrative?
5. How should a method stop infinite recursive analysis?
6. Which real case and ethical evaluation would provide the sharpest test?

## Review packets

Chess rankings; newsroom/platform distribution; dating platform incentives; worker decision rights; municipal service maintenance. Each packet must separate observed facts, hypotheses, normative assumptions and proposed interventions.

## Deliverables

An annotated critique, recommended primary sources, one procedural improvement, one counterexample and one observable success/failure criterion. Preserve disagreements and attribution.

## Collaboration model

Research track refines claims; practitioner track pilots techniques; educational track tests teachability. Applications feed revisions without retroactively proving the thesis.
