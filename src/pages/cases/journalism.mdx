# One system. Six analytical views.

This interactive teaching case explores a **hypothetical** relationship between journalists, a distribution platform, and audiences. It does not claim to reconstruct any actual platform's algorithm.

<CaseExplorer client:load />

## What changes as you switch views?

The participants stay the same. Their relevant **relationships, mechanisms, evidence questions and intervention levers** change. That is the central distinction System Canvas aims to teach: a system is not adequately explained by a single diagram.

## Try a critical intervention

In the explorer, choose a lens and then select an intervention. Observe how the *hypothesized* effect and its potential side effects vary. These are **qualitative conjectures**, not a simulation or evidence that the intervention works.

See the [practitioner field guide](/docs/system-canvas-field-guide/) for a full case worksheet and the [validation protocol](/research/system-canvas-validation-protocol/) for how this could become a genuine research case.
