## Essential cautionary distinctions

System vs game; composition vs containment; recursion vs second-order cybernetics; statement vs evidence; credibility vs truth; knowing vs believing; declared purpose vs incentive vs effect; norm vs enforcement; authority vs legitimacy; survival vs desirability; power-over vs power-to; useful diagrams vs demonstrated causal mechanisms.

## Maturity process

**Concept → testable hypothesis → repeatable procedure → pilot evidence → comparative validation → teachable mature method.**

## Open research questions

- Which cross-view questions find otherwise missed mechanisms?

- What evidence differentiates causal mechanisms from stories?
- How can plural goals be negotiated rather than presumed?
- How can analysts account for externalities and nonhuman ecological systems?
- How do interventions alter the incentives and power of their own regulators?
- Which classroom exercise transfers to unfamiliar domains?
