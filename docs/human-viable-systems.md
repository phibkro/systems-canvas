# Human Viable Systems

**The related normative research program: viable for whom, under what conditions, and at whose expense?**

Human Viable Systems (HVS) explores how organizational and institutional arrangements can sustain themselves **while supporting human agency, meaningful participation, legitimate governance, social well-being and ecological sustainability**. It draws on organizational cybernetics and viable systems, ecological economics, institutional analysis and critical perspectives on alienation and concentrated authority.

This is a **research orientation and developing model**, not an empirically proven universal welfare function.

## Three different questions

| Question | Lens or framework |
|---|---|
| How does an enterprise operate and regulate itself? | [Enterprise Model](/docs/enterprise-model/) |
| Why do its agents choose certain actions? | [Game Model](/docs/game-model/) |
| Is its operation and continued existence desirable, viable and legitimate **for those affected**? | Human Viable Systems evaluation |

**Viability ≠ desirability.** A company or institution may maintain itself through profitable, stable processes that undermine workers, communities or ecological systems. Evaluation should not treat organizational survival as inherently good.

## Core commitments to investigate

- **Human agency:** Are people doing the work able to meaningfully participate in decisions affecting them? Distinguish agents from stakeholders, and identify overlaps.
- **External sustainability:** Are ecological capacities, finite resources, social relationships or other enterprises depleted or harmed by the undertaking?
- **Internal sustainability:** Can essential capabilities, livelihoods, learning, coordination and adaptability be maintained without destructive conditions?
- **Legitimacy and distribution:** Who benefits, who bears risks and costs, who sets the goals, and who can contest the rules?
- **Feedback and revision:** Does evidence about harmful outcomes reach people with authority and the ability to correct them?

Different evaluators may disagree about values or priorities. The method should **declare criteria explicitly**, retain disagreements, and separate normative commitments from empirical descriptions.

## How it relates to Enterprise & Agency Modeling

[Enterprise & Agency Modeling](/docs/enterprise-agency-modeling/) supplies representations and diagnostic procedures. HVS offers one set of **explicit evaluation commitments**, not a required assumption in every model. We must also be able to model exploitative enterprises accurately or investigate institutions whose declared goals are contested.

AI agents can be participants in enterprise and game models. This does **not** automatically grant AI agents the same moral standing as humans; their permissions, accountability, principals, and effects on human stakeholders must be specified separately.

## Prior art and research status

Stafford Beer's Viable System Model, Raúl Espejo's Enterprise Complexity Model, Elinor Ostrom's institutional analysis, participation and governance research, and ecological economics are important foundations. These are not inventions of HVS. The proposed contribution is to connect descriptive modeling with explicit human- and ecology-oriented evaluation, and test whether the combined method improves actual diagnosis and intervention.
